## INTRODUCTION

This proposal outlines a comprehensive consulting engagement to support informed decision-making for residential property acquisition, condominium development, and legal risk management in Kampala's premium and mid-market residential nodes.

The engagement comprises three complementary consulting workstreams:

1. **Residential Market Analysis** — pricing, demand, yields, and investment positioning
2. **Development Feasibility Assessment** — land costs, approvals, construction, and infrastructure
3. **Legal Due Diligence and Buyer Protection Framework** — title verification, tenure systems, and transaction protection

Each workstream is designed to be delivered independently or as an integrated package, depending on the client's development stage and decision-making needs.

# WORKSTREAM 1: RESIDENTIAL MARKET ANALYSIS

## 1.1 Assignment Title
**Residential Market Analysis for Selected Kampala Nodes: Kololo, Bugolobi, Muyenga, and Makindye Division**

## 1.2 Background
The client is evaluating residential acquisition and investment opportunities within selected Kampala neighborhoods and requires a decision-grade market assessment covering pricing benchmarks, rental performance, buyer and tenant demand, transaction costs, and key market risks.

## 1.3 Objective
To provide a comparative residential market report that supports informed decision-making for:
- direct residential property acquisition,
- off-plan apartment purchases,
- rental-income investment strategies, and
- medium-term capital appreciation positioning.

## 1.4 Scope of Services
The consultant shall:

- assess the residential market positioning of **Kololo, Bugolobi, Muyenga, and Makindye Division**;
- compile current pricing benchmarks for **off-plan and completed residential apartments**;
- analyze recent historical and current trends in **capital appreciation and rental yields**;
- identify target buyer and tenant demographics in each node;
- itemize transaction costs, taxes, and market-entry friction relevant to residential investment in Uganda;
- assess key investment, liquidity, and execution risks affecting each location; and
- rank the selected locations by investment attractiveness and buyer suitability.

## 1.5 Methodology
The consultant will apply a mixed-method market research approach:

- desktop market research
- broker, developer, and listing comparison
- rental comparable analysis
- transaction cost benchmarking
- qualitative node assessment
- source triangulation where hard market data is fragmented or inconsistent

## 1.6 Deliverables
The consultant shall submit:

1. **Inception Note / Research Framework**
2. **Comparative Residential Market Report** (15–25 pages)
3. **Pricing and Yield Benchmark Tables** (Excel)
4. **Investor Recommendation Summary** (1-page executive brief)
5. **Executive Presentation Deck** *(optional, additional fee)*

## 1.7 Expected Report Contents
The final market report shall include:

- Executive Summary (investment highlights and key risks)
- Methodology and Key Assumptions
- Node-by-Node Market Analysis
  - Kololo
  - Bugolobi
  - Muyenga
  - Makindye Division
- Price per Square Meter Benchmarks (off-plan and completed)
- Capital Appreciation and Rental Yield Assessment
- Buyer and Tenant Demographic Analysis
- Transaction Cost and Tax Schedule
- Risk Matrix and Red Flags
- Location Ranking and Investment Recommendations
- Data Sources and Confidence Notes

## 1.8 Deliverable Format
Deliverables shall be provided as:

- editable document format (Word or Google Docs)
- PDF report
- Excel-compatible benchmark tables

# WORKSTREAM 3: LEGAL DUE DILIGENCE AND BUYER PROTECTION FRAMEWORK

## 3.1 Assignment Title
**Legal Due Diligence and Buyer Protection Framework for Off-Plan and Residential Property Acquisition in Kampala**

## 3.2 Background
The client intends to acquire off-plan property, condominium units, or land for residential development in Kampala and requires a legal and due diligence framework to reduce title risk, developer risk, and transaction-structure risk.

## 3.3 Objective
To provide a practical legal roadmap and actionable due diligence checklist for residential acquisitions in **Kololo, Bugolobi, Muyenga, and Makindye Division**, with a focus on:
- legal title verification and risk assessment
- tenure system analysis
- developer and counterparty protection
- payment structuring and escrow arrangements
- transaction documentation requirements
- post-closing compliance

## 3.4 Scope of Services
The consultant shall:

- explain the applicable land tenure systems (Mailo, Freehold, Leasehold) and their transaction implications;
- review the legal framework governing condominium property ownership and title issuance;
- provide a step-by-step title verification and land due diligence workflow;
- advise on off-plan sale agreement protections and milestone-based payment controls;
- identify the key legal and transactional documents a prudent buyer should request;
- produce a red-flag checklist and chronological buyer action plan; and
- provide guidance on escrow arrangements, stakeholder protection, and default remedies.

## 3.5 Methodology
The consultant will undertake:

- legal and regulatory review (Ugandan land law, tenure systems, Condominium Property Act)
- title transfer and registration workflow analysis
- transaction-structure assessment
- buyer-protection contract analysis
- practical risk mapping based on Kampala conveyancing realities
- document review and due diligence protocols

## 3.6 Deliverables
The consultant shall submit:

1. **Legal Framework Report** (12–18 pages)
2. **Due Diligence Checklist** (detailed, step-by-step)
3. **Off-Plan Buyer Protection Note** (1-page summary)
4. **Document Request Checklist** (comprehensive list)
5. **Red-Flag and Risk Memo** (warning signs to pause or withdraw)
6. **Draft Heads of Terms / Protective Clause Checklist** *(optional, additional fee)*

## 3.7 Expected Report Contents
The legal framework report shall include:

- Executive Legal Summary
- Tenure System Analysis
  - Mailo land (definition, risks, transfer mechanics)
  - Freehold (definition, risks, transfer mechanics)
  - Leasehold (definition, risks, transfer mechanics)
  - Tenure prevalence by node (Kololo, Bugolobi, Muyenga, Makindye)
- Condominium Property Law Overview
  - creation and subdivision of units
  - title issuance process
  - common area ownership and management
  - buyer protections under Ugandan law
- Title Verification Checklist
  - search process
  - encumbrance review
  - boundary verification
  - planning compliance
- Escrow and Milestone-Payment Protection Framework
  - stakeholder account structures
  - payment release conditions
  - architect/engineer certification requirements
  - default remedies
- Required Documents List
- Red Flags and Risk Indicators
- Chronological Transaction Roadmap
- Data Sources and Legal Confidence Notes

## 3.8 Deliverable Format
Deliverables shall be provided as:

- PDF memo/report
- editable checklist document
- optional annexure containing recommended protective clause structures
